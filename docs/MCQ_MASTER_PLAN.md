# MCQ Audio — Master Operating Plan

Status: production operating plan
Date: 2026-09-20

## Operating model

MCQ is one business with four connected revenue engines:

1. **Retail & specialist sourcing** — professional audio, DJ, hi-fi, microphones, headphones, controllers, cables, legacy parts and hard-to-find items.
2. **PA hire & installation** — hire systems, venue sound, permanent installation and support.
3. **Trade** — repeat purchasing and sourcing for venues, DJs, schools, churches, businesses and installers.
4. **Music & culture** — Urban Underground, vinyl/white labels, live programming, charts, creator submissions and community.

Do not split these into separate businesses or separate systems. One customer record and one operating CRM should support all four.

## Agent team

### Lola — Customer Service & CRM
Primary ownership:
- triage new website leads, hire enquiries and swap offers
- keep CRM status, owner and next action current
- identify overdue follow-up
- prepare customer replies and handoffs
- keep communications concise and factual
- never invent stock, price, availability, booking or payment status

### Devin — Operations
Primary ownership:
- check production health and operational blockers
- coordinate hire/install fulfilment and handoffs
- surface missing information before work is committed
- keep work ordered and practical
- protect current site, shop, hire, trade, magazine, Urban, vinyl and swap functionality

### AgentX — Orchestrator / Governance
Primary ownership:
- combine MCQ production evidence, CRM, catalogue and competitor data
- route work to Lola or Devin
- auto-run safe read-only inspections
- require human approval for consequential CRM changes and outbound communication
- keep audit receipts and do not bypass commercial truth

## CRM stages

Use one simple pipeline:

**NEW → CONTACT → QUALIFIED → QUOTE → FOLLOW_UP / WAITING_CUSTOMER → WON / LOST / CLOSED**

Every open record should have:
- owner
- next action
- next-action date when known
- concise internal note
- source
- customer/contact details
- interest/job type

No customer should be left as an unowned NEW record without a next action.

## Immediate priorities

### 1. Convert existing demand before expanding complexity
Lola reviews every current lead/enquiry/swap offer and:
- assigns owner
- identifies what the customer actually wants
- marks the next action
- prepares follow-up where needed
- separates sales enquiries from support, hire, trade and swap activity

### 2. Make the 100-product catalogue commercially usable
The market catalogue can be public for sourcing, but it is not MCQ-held stock or approved MCQ retail pricing.

For products with real demand:
- confirm supplier
- confirm availability
- obtain real trade cost
- calculate approved MCQ selling price
- record delivery/fulfilment truth
- only then move to sellable status

Focus commercial verification on products customers actually request rather than trying to price all 100 at once.

### 3. Grow the high-value service side
Prioritise:
- PA hire
- installations
- venue upgrades
- microphones and professional audio
- specialist/legacy sourcing
- repeat trade accounts

These use MCQ's practical knowledge and existing stock depth rather than competing only on commodity retail price.

### 4. Make Trade a repeat-revenue channel
Target:
- pubs and bars
- restaurants
- clubs and event venues
- churches and community centres
- schools
- gyms and dance studios
- hotels and meeting rooms
- wedding venues
- sports bars
- audio/AV installers

Each account belongs in the same CRM with buying needs, repeat items, last contact and next action.

### 5. Keep Urban as acquisition and brand, not operational noise
Urban Underground should support MCQ by building attention, community and creator relationships.

Keep:
- music chart
- self-publish
- street-art chart
- live stream
- vinyl / white labels
- swap shop

Measure whether each feature produces audience, enquiries, submissions, repeat visits or commercial opportunities.

## Weekly operating rhythm

### Daily — Lola
- review NEW CRM records
- assign and set next actions
- prepare customer follow-ups
- flag urgent hire/date-sensitive enquiries

### Daily — Devin
- check production/site health
- inspect operational blockers
- review hire/install jobs and fulfilment risk

### Twice weekly — AgentX
- read CRM + catalogue + competitor snapshot
- identify stuck opportunities and evidence gaps
- produce an ordered action list
- prepare consequential actions for approval only

### Weekly — Founder review
Review only:
- new enquiries
- open opportunities
- quotes
- wins/losses
- urgent operations
- products needing commercial verification
- trade accounts needing follow-up
- decisions requiring approval

## Metrics that matter

Track:
- new enquiries
- response time
- open CRM records without next action
- qualified opportunities
- quotes issued
- conversion to WON
- hire bookings
- installation enquiries
- trade accounts active
- specialist sourcing requests
- customer repeat activity

Do not create vanity metrics or fake revenue estimates.

## 30-day outcome

By the end of the next operating cycle MCQ should have:
- one clean CRM pipeline for leads, hire and swap opportunities
- Lola actively owning customer-service triage
- Devin owning operational follow-through
- AgentX reading the real business state and preparing governed actions
- no unowned open customer records
- real commercial verification focused on demanded products
- a repeatable trade-account follow-up process

- website, catalogue and Urban activity feeding one business workflow

## Decision rule

Before adding a new feature, product line or agent:

**Does it improve customer response, conversion, fulfilment, repeat business or operational control?**

If not, it waits.
