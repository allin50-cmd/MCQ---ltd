# MCQ Production Agent Rules

MCQ is an established 55-year audio business. Treat it as a live business system, not a demo.

## Non-negotiable scope
- Preserve client-requested capabilities. Improve or stage them; never silently remove them.
- Urban Gallery / street art, Urban Underground, Urban Chart, Live, Vinyl / White Labels, Swap Shop, Shop Audio, specialist legacy stock, Hire & Install, Trade, Magazine and About remain in scope.
- Existing working APIs and business flows are grafted and improved before replacement.

## Product trust gate
A product may be rendered publicly only when:
1. the product/model is identified,
2. the image URL is present and loads,
3. the image is high-enough resolution for the surface,
4. the image corresponds to the stated product/model,
5. price/stock claims are verified or the CTA clearly says ask/reserve/source.

Never ship:
- broken image icons,
- blank product-image boxes,
- low-resolution thumbnails stretched into hero media,
- generated lookalikes presented as branded products,
- a different model used as a substitute.

If the image gate fails, the product stays hidden/DRAFT until corrected.

## Visual direction
High-end audio editorial × London club culture × street art × MCQ heritage.
Avoid generic SaaS/e-commerce template styling.

## Evidence states
Use only:
- CODED
- TESTED
- DEPLOYED
- LIVE-VERIFIED

Do not call work complete from a green build alone.

## Production closeout
Every significant change must report:
- what changed,
- what was tested,
- deployed commit,
- live routes verified,
- remaining blockers.
