CULTURE MEETS EQUIPMENT

The record and the machine belong on the same website.

A collector discovering a white label may need a stylus. A DJ buying a cartridge may discover the archive. That connection is the commercial advantage of MCQ owning both music and audio.

RIGHTS BEFORE RELEASE

Possessing a record is not the same as owning the master.

Every reissue or digital release needs a documented rights position before production. The website can celebrate the archive without implying MCQ has permission to commercially re-release every title in it.

Archive first. Rights second. Release only when both are clear.
