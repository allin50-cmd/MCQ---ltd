MCQ BUYING PHILOSOPHY

Price is not the same as value.

Entry-level equipment should be easy to understand and hard to regret. Professional equipment should survive work. Premium equipment should solve a real problem rather than just display a bigger badge.

Spend where the system is weak. Save where the difference is cosmetic.

That approach keeps MCQ useful to a teenager buying their first controller, a venue replacing microphones, and a serious listener upgrading a complete system.
