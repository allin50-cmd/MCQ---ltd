MCQ EXPLAINER

Bluetooth is a connection. The system is everything around it.

Battery, codec support, stereo pairing, room correction, analogue inputs and amplifier/speaker integration determine whether a product is a travel speaker or a real hi-fi system.

BUYING GUIDE

What actually matters.

Portable

Prioritise battery, weight, protection and usable output before chasing audiophile language.

Party

Look for sustained output, microphone/guitar inputs, stereo pairing and battery behaviour at high level.

Home wireless

Wi‑Fi streaming, HDMI, phono, room correction and app quality matter more than portability.

Stereo

A pair of speakers placed correctly will usually create a more convincing soundstage than a single Bluetooth box.
