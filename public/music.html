WHY THIS EXISTS

Not nostalgia.
Living underground culture.

Vinyl Underground should be the place where the MCQ shop’s history, DJ knowledge and UK Garage catalogue become a commercial advantage — not a box of stock hidden behind the audio business.

Collectors can dig. DJs can find cuts. New listeners can discover the catalogue. Sellers can bring records back into circulation. Exclusive pressings create reasons to return.

THE CATALOGUE

Dig by sound, format or release.

Real MCQ catalogue data only. No fabricated releases or fake stock.

WHITE LABEL PROGRAMME

Press it again.
Drop it first.

Two lanes: carefully selected reissues from the catalogue, and limited pre-release white labels for DJs and collectors before general release.

01

Archive Reissue

Bring sought-after catalogue cuts back properly: clear rights, new master where needed, controlled pressing, numbered run if appropriate.

02

Exclusive White Label

MCQ-only physical edition. Strong sleeve/label system, provenance, catalogue number and collector story.

03

Pre-Release

DJ-first drop before wider release. Small run, timed window, early access list, strict quantity controls.

04

Digital Companion

Where rights allow, pair physical releases with MP3/high-quality digital so the catalogue works for collectors and working DJs.

MP3 / DIGITAL

The catalogue without the crate.

Digital availability is driven by rights and the MCQ catalogue record, not assumed automatically.
