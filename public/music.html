WHY THIS EXISTS

Not one genre.
The next sound.

Urban Underground is the open front door for bedroom DJs, producers, singers, rappers, beat makers and independent labels. Garage remains part of MCQ’s heritage, but the platform follows what people are actually making now.

The Urban Chart is not editorially selected and has no paid ranking. Creators self-publish; listeners decide what rises through unique support votes.

PURE USER INPUT

The Urban Chart.

Unsigned. Independent. Pre-pre-release. No label gatekeeper, no paid chart position, no MCQ editorial ranking. One unique support vote per listener email per track.

SELF PUBLISH

Made it tonight?
Put it in front of people tonight.

Upload your identity, artwork and a playable preview URL. Choose the stage honestly: Pre‑Pre Release, Unsigned or Independent. Your submission goes into the public Urban Chart immediately once you confirm you control the rights.

MCQ does not take ownership of your music. Publishing here is a showcase/chart submission, not a record deal.

WHITE LABEL PROGRAMME

Press it again.
Drop it first.

Two lanes: carefully selected reissues from the catalogue, and limited pre-release white labels for DJs and collectors before general release.

01

Archive Reissue

Bring sought-after catalogue cuts back properly: clear rights, new master where needed, controlled pressing, numbered run if appropriate.

02

Exclusive White Label

MCQ-only physical edition. Strong sleeve/label system, provenance, catalogue number and collector story.

03

Pre-Release

DJ-first drop before wider release. Small run, timed window, early access list, strict quantity controls.

04

Digital Companion

Where rights allow, pair physical releases with MP3/high-quality digital so the catalogue works for collectors and working DJs.

MP3 / DIGITAL
