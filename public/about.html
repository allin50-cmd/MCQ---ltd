THE BUSINESS

Knowledge was always the valuable inventory.

Audio retail changes. Formats change. Manufacturers change. What survives is knowing what connects to what, what fails in the real world, what a DJ needs five minutes before a set, and what a venue should have bought the first time.

MCQ's modernisation should not erase the old business. It should turn that accumulated knowledge into searchable catalogue, buying guides, trade sourcing, hire systems and installation expertise.

Keep the corporate trust. Let the culture show people why MCQ is different.
