Wireless convenience and hi-fi are no longer opposites.

Codec support, driver design, DSP, fit, amplification and isolation all shape the result. The right headphone depends on where and how you listen.

BUYING GUIDE

What actually matters.

Closed vs open

Closed headphones isolate and travel better. Open designs usually trade isolation for a more spacious presentation.

Bluetooth codecs

Codec choice matters, but transducer quality, DSP and fit can matter more than the badge on the box.

ANC

Noise cancelling is invaluable on trains and flights but not automatically desirable for a quiet dedicated listening room.

Drive requirements

Sensitivity and impedance determine whether a phone, laptop or dedicated headphone amp is the sensible partner.
