HOW IT WORKS

Send details first

Tell MCQ the exact model, condition, faults and what is included. MCQ will assess it before making any offer. No automatic valuation is promised.

WHY IT WORKS

Customers become suppliers.

A collection submitted to MCQ can become stock, editorial, archive knowledge and new reasons for collectors to return. The Swap Shop is not a charity bin; it is a circular inventory engine. MCQ can assess one item or a larger collection, but an offer is only made after the actual item and condition are reviewed.

Submit a collection

APPRAISAL

Condition + demand + provenance.

A famous title in poor condition may be less valuable than an obscure promo in excellent condition. MCQ should record catalogue numbers, pressing details, sleeve condition and provenance before making strong value claims.

Large collections should be treated as a separate workflow from single-record submissions because transport, sorting time and dead stock change the economics.
