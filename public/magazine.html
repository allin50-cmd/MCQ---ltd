EDITORIAL RULE

We separate specification, opinion and evidence.

A manufacturer specification is useful. A reviewer's experience is useful. Marketing language can also be useful — if it is labelled as marketing rather than presented as fact.

MCQ Magazine should help the reader understand the product, not perform intelligence at them.

That editorial discipline is especially important when the magazine also sells equipment. Trust is worth more than pretending every new product is revolutionary.
