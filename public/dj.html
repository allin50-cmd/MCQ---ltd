BUYING GUIDE

What actually matters.

Entry controller

Two channels, familiar layout and software compatibility are enough to learn phrasing, gain structure, EQ and transitions.

Portable standalone

Battery power and built-in screens remove the laptop and make mobile parties genuinely practical.

Performance controller

Four channels, stems, deeper effects and club-style layout reward experienced DJs and mobile professionals.

All-in-one flagship

Standalone players justify their cost when reliability, professional outputs and club-style workflow matter more than portability.
