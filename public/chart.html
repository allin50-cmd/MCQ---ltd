HOW IT WORKS

People decide what moves.

A creator self-publishes a track and confirms they control the preview rights. Listeners can support a track once per email address. The chart orders tracks by unique support count, with newer submissions breaking ties.

No pay-to-chart. Ever.

MCQ can sell services around the ecosystem — equipment, pressing, mastering partners, events and promotion products — but ranking position remains separate from commercial spend.
