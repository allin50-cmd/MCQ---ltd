BUYING GUIDE

What actually matters.

Dynamic vs condenser

Dynamic mics are usually tougher and less sensitive to room noise; condensers can reveal more detail but demand better gain structure and environment.

Polar pattern

Cardioid is forgiving. Supercardioid can give stronger rejection but needs more careful monitor and performer placement.

Wireless

Spend more for spectrum management, receiver quality, battery systems and reliability — not simply to remove a cable.

Gain before feedback

Capsule choice, placement and loudspeaker geometry work together. The mic alone cannot solve a bad PA layout.
