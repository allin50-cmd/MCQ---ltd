MCQ / PEOPLE • MUSIC • PLACES

The city is
part of the sound.

Street art is not decoration around MCQ Urban Underground. It is part of the visual culture around the music: walls, people, lettering, protest, colour, memory and the neighbourhoods where scenes grow.

Art sets the tone.
People set the culture.

MCQ's Urban Gallery connects visual artists, DJs, producers, collectors and audiences without turning street culture into a generic design theme. The gallery can expand with credited work from artists who choose to participate.
