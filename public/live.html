SHOW FORMAT

Established culture meets what has not happened yet.

The live show is not only a nostalgia vehicle. Its job is to create a bridge between recognised names, MCQ's archive and music being made in bedrooms right now.

01

Host Sets

DJ-led opening sets that establish the tone of each edition.

02

Guest Session

Conversation, performance and stories from urban music culture.

03

Chart Picks

Unsigned and pre-pre-release tracks submitted through Urban Underground.

04

Archive

White labels, vinyl, catalogue records and records that explain where the sound came from.

THE IMPORTANT BIT

The live show feeds the chart. The chart feeds the live show.

A bedroom producer can publish today, gain support from real listeners, submit the track to the next stream, and potentially be heard beside established guests without buying a chart position.
